### What Was Accomplished 🎨
We conducted a comprehensive redesign pass of the NXRTH sales page to make it calm, spacious, and effortlessly scannable for furniture shop owners:

1. **Strict 8-Screen Structure Applied**:
   - **Screen 1 (Hero):** Centered display headline, one clean subline, primary and secondary buttons, one large CSS browser frame mockup with an overlapping chat bubble, and unobtrusive 10px cursor.
   - **Screen 2 (Statement):** Breathing room screen with pure focus: *"Exceptional furniture craft. Yet invisible online."*
   - **Screen 3 (Work):** Left-aligned `WORK` label, 2 large clean browser-frame cards for Jeet Furniture and Best Furniture Store with a direct *"Visit site"* link.
   - **Screen 4 (AI Assistant):** Left-aligned `AI ASSISTANT` label, interactive scripted demo with 3 tappable questions, and single *"Start a project"* CTA.
   - **Screen 5 (How It Works):** Big lime numerals (`01`, `02`, `03`) with zero bounding boxes.
   - **Screen 6 (Pricing):** 2 clean plan cards with Template highlighted as primary; 3 succinct bullets each; single muted explainer line beneath.
   - **Screen 7 (FAQ):** 4 clean collapsed questions directly on the background in a 720px column.
   - **Screen 8 (Final CTA & Footer):** Centered headline and subline, Start Flow panel showing question 1 with 5 clean pills, and a solid dark footer with pixel-terrain dither contained to a 35% opacity band behind the giant wordmark.

2. **Word Count Budget**:
   - Total visible page copy is exactly **410 words** (well within the 350–450 target).
   - Removed all jargon (SLA, CDN, edge, concierge, architecture, dominance, white-glove).
   - Removed all invented stats and fake percentage numbers.

3. **Cleanup**:
   - Deleted unused section components: `marquee.tsx`, `services-grid.tsx`, `problem-promise.tsx`, `chatbot-cta-strip.tsx`, `chatbot-showcase.tsx`, `templates-preview.tsx`.
   - Deleted unused data files: `services.ts`, `templates.ts`.

4. **Zero Git Action Taken**:
